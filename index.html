01 / ABOUT

Built from the things that should have broken us.

BlackGlass Saints is a dark alternative rock / alt-metal project fronted by recording artist Nyka Snow.

The project lives where heavy guitars, haunting atmosphere and emotionally raw storytelling collide. The music is already being written and recorded. Now the next chapter is becoming physical: a performance-ready live band built to turn those songs into something bigger than the room.

This is not a casual jam project. The goal is a serious live identity, strong visual world, original releases, collaboration and stages worth remembering.
